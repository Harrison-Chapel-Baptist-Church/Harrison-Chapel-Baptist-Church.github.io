Harrison Chapel for Android

Stay connected with your church family from your phone.

Download Android App

Official APK hosted through the church's GitHub release. If a download is unavailable, contact church administration.

How to install

  1. Tap the download button.
  2. Open the downloaded APK on your Android phone.
  3. Allow installation from your browser or file manager if Android requests it.
  4. Install and sign in with your Harrison Chapel account.
  5. Disable the temporary installation permission afterward.

Only install APKs from the church's official release.

Church Member Access

Sign in with your existing Harrison Chapel account.

New members can register through the Android app. Your website account is the same account.
